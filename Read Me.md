CVMax is a private, offline resume builder that never asks for an account, never shows ads, and never sends your information anywhere. Your career story stays exactly where it belongs — on your phone.

WHY CVMAX
- No sign-up, no login, no cloud sync — start building immediately
- 100% offline: CVMax works with no internet connection and requests no internet permission
- Nothing is uploaded, tracked, or sold. Your data stays in the app's private storage on your device

A GUIDED, STRESS-FREE WORKFLOW
CVMax walks you through five clear steps — Details, Template, Preview, Confirm, and Export — so nothing gets forgotten. Add your personal details, professional summary, work experience, education, skills, certifications, languages, references, and custom sections at your own pace, with everything autosaved as you go.

13 PROFESSIONAL TEMPLATES
Swipe through a gallery of 13 original, thoughtfully designed templates spanning modern, minimalist, corporate, creative, elegant, editorial, and sidebar styles — so your resume looks the part whether you're applying for a corporate role or a creative one.

MULTIPLE RESUMES, FULLY UNDER YOUR CONTROL
Save as many resumes as you need for different roles or industries. Duplicate a resume to quickly create a tailored variant, or delete the ones you no longer need — all stored locally, all in your control.

FIX MY CV
CVMax includes a one-tap, fully offline "Fix My CV" tool that reviews your experience bullets, suggests stronger and more factual phrasing, and can infer a suitable professional profile for you. Every suggested change is shown as a clear before-and-after comparison, and role-typical responsibility suggestions are always confirmed separately before anything is applied — you stay in charge of every word.

EXPORT YOUR WAY
When you're ready, export a polished PDF in A4 or US Letter size. Save it directly to your device through Android's document picker, or share it straight to email, messaging, or cloud apps of your choice — CVMax simply hands off the finished file; it doesn't upload anything itself.

PROFILE PHOTO, YOUR CHOICE
Add a profile photo using Android's built-in document picker. CVMax only ever accesses the single photo you choose — never your full photo library.

LIGHT, DARK, OR SYSTEM THEME
Use CVMax comfortably day or night, or let it follow your device's theme automatically.

BUILT DIFFERENTLY, ON PURPOSE
CVMax is built using only standard Android platform APIs, with no third-party runtime libraries, no analytics, and no ad networks. It's a resume builder built the way a privacy-conscious tool should be: simple, local, and entirely yours.

Whether you're applying for your first job or your next big career move, CVMax helps you put your best, most polished resume forward — privately.
