Privacy Policy for CVMax Last updated: [18/08/2026]

CVMax is an offline resume-building app. It does not have internet access permission and cannot send data anywhere.

No account or sign-in. CVMax does not ask you to register or log in.
No data collection. CVMax does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, analytics, or usage data.
No ads or trackers. CVMax contains no advertising or analytics software development kits (SDKs).
Local storage only. Your resumes, personal details, and any photo you choose are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. Nothing leaves your phone unless you personally choose to export or share a PDF using Android's built-in save/share tools.
Photo access. If you add a profile photo, CVMax opens Android's system document picker. CVMax never gains broad access to your photo library — only the specific image you select.
Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored resume data from your device.
